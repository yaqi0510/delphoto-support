1.0 发布说明

适用版本：1.0（构建 4）首发提交 · 说明整理日期：2026-10-06。App Store 上架状态请以商店显示为准。

用生活中的一点片刻，重温相册里的美好片刻。

片刻把相册整理拆成轻松的小组，让你在随机回看照片与视频时，顺手留下珍贵回忆、整理不再需要的内容。

首发功能

  • 分组回看：图片与视频分开整理，跨日期随机挑选内容；按媒体分类或相册筛选，可设置每组数量，默认跳过已看过的项目。
  • 先标记，再删除：左右翻页，上滑标记，支持撤销；组末集中复核，经过系统确认才删除，取消或失败时保留待删除清单。
  • 随时继续：在本机保存浏览进度、筛选设置、已看过记录和待删除标记，下次打开继续整理。
  • 重温那一天：从当前内容进入附近照片墙，查看时间相邻的照片或视频，缩放浏览并将标记带回本组复核。
  • 完整浏览体验：支持照片缩放、Live Photo 播放与重播、GIF、视频进度和静音控制，以及横屏播放。
  • 详情与分享：查看相册记录的时间、文件信息、尺寸和地点信息；通过系统分享面板分享本地资源，Live Photo 以静态照片分享。
  • 记录整理成果：查看今天、近 7 天、近 30 天和累计统计，包括已查看、已删除及预计可释放空间。
  • 本机处理与隐私：无需注册账号，不上传图库，不含广告或分析上报；App 内可离线阅读隐私政策，未授权相册时也可查看。

使用说明

需要 iOS 18 或更高版本，适用于 iPhone。片刻只请求本地媒体，不主动下载 iCloud 原片；仅云端可用或无法读取的项目可能被跳过。开启 iCloud 照片时，系统删除可能同步到其他设备；恢复期限以系统“最近删除”为准，预计可释放空间不等于实际腾出的设备空间。

本说明对应已上传的首发构建。后续共用设置、中英文切换、联系邮箱和在线政策链接更新未计入此构建的发布说明。

DelPhoto 1.0 — Release Notes

Applies to the initial 1.0 submission, build 4. Notes prepared on October 6, 2026. Check the App Store for availability.

Make a little time for the moments in your library.

DelPhoto turns photo cleanup into small batches, helping you rediscover memories while deciding what to keep.

  • Review in small batches: Organize photos and videos separately, revisit media from different dates, filter by category or album, and choose a batch size. Previously viewed items are skipped by default.
  • Mark, review, then delete: Swipe between items, swipe up to mark, and undo when needed. Review the batch before the system asks you to confirm deletion. Cancelled or failed deletions keep the pending list.
  • Pick up where you left off: Filters, browsing progress, viewed records, and pending marks are saved on your device.
  • Revisit nearby moments: Open a nearby-media wall from the current item, browse adjacent moments, adjust the view size, and bring marks back to the batch review.
  • Browse your media: Zoom into photos, play and replay Live Photos, view GIFs, control video playback and sound, and watch videos in landscape.
  • Details and sharing: View the time, file information, dimensions, and location recorded with an item. Share local resources through the system share sheet; Live Photos are shared as still images.
  • See your progress: View daily, 7-day, 30-day, and all-time totals for reviewed items, deleted items, and estimated reclaimable space.
  • On-device processing and privacy: No account, library uploads, advertising, or analytics. The privacy policy is available offline, even without photo-library permission.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. DelPhoto does not download iCloud originals, so cloud-only or unreadable media may be skipped. With iCloud Photos enabled, the system may sync deletions to other devices. Recovery follows the time shown in Recently Deleted; estimated space may differ from actual storage reclaimed.

These notes describe the uploaded initial build. Later shared-settings, in-app language, contact-email, and online-policy-link updates are not included in this build's release notes.

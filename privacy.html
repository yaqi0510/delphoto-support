隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月5日 · 开发者：Yaqi Dai

本机处理

片刻无需注册账号。照片、视频和整理记录在本机处理；App 不向开发者上传媒体、相册信息、位置或使用记录，不包含广告、分析上报或第三方跟踪 SDK。

相册权限与用途

在你授权后，片刻读取允许访问的照片、视频及相关信息，用于分类、预览、播放、显示详情、估算大小和整理删除。详情可能包含照片已有的拍摄时间、文件名、尺寸和经纬度；App 不获取设备的实时位置，也不进行在线地理编码。你可以仅授权部分照片，并随时在系统设置中调整或撤销权限。

本地记录与保留

筛选设置、语言偏好、浏览进度、已看过记录、待删除标记及统计保存在 App 的本地存储中，供下次继续整理。删除 App 会移除其本地记录；系统备份及恢复由你的设备设置和 Apple 服务管理。撤销相册权限不会自动清除已有整理记录。

删除与 iCloud 照片

上滑或点击删除只会标记内容。完成组末复核并通过系统确认后，才执行删除；取消或失败会保留待删除清单。片刻不主动下载 iCloud 原片。若开启 iCloud 照片，系统仍可能将删除同步到其他设备；找回期限以系统“最近删除”显示为准。

分享与临时文件

只有你主动分享时，片刻才准备本地媒体并打开系统分享面板；接收方和发送操作由你选择。Live Photo 以静态照片分享，媒体可能包含原有元数据，请在发送前检查。分享结束或取消后会清理本次临时文件；进程被强制结束时，临时文件可能留待系统清理。接收 App 或服务按各自的隐私政策处理内容。

联系与反馈

开发者联系邮箱为 574700790@qq.com。App 的“联系与反馈”按钮只复制联系地址，不会自动发送邮件；如旧版本显示其他地址，请以本页公布的邮箱为准。你主动发送的邮件内容和联系方式将用于处理反馈，请勿发送不必要的私人照片或敏感信息。如需查询或请求删除反馈邮件中的个人信息，可通过同一邮箱联系；必要的服务、安全及法律记录可能需要保留。

本网站

本支持网站不设置分析或广告工具，也没有照片上传功能。访问网页时，托管服务 GitHub Pages 会处理提供网页所需的网络请求信息，例如 IP 地址和浏览器请求信息；这与 App 在本机处理相册是不同的流程。通过邮箱发送的信息还由邮件服务提供商按其政策处理。

Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026 · Developer: Yaqi Dai

On-device processing

DelPhoto (片刻) requires no account. The app processes photos, videos, and cleanup records on your device. It does not upload your media, photo library information, location, or usage records to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Photo access

With your permission, the app reads accessible photos, videos, and related information to filter, preview, play, display details, estimate sizes, and delete selected items. Details can include existing capture times, filenames, dimensions, and coordinates. The app does not request your live location or use online geocoding. You can grant limited photo access or change and revoke access in iOS Settings.

Local records and retention

Filters, language preferences, progress, viewed-item records, pending deletion marks, and statistics are stored locally so you can continue later. Deleting the app removes its local records. Device backups and restores are managed by your device settings and Apple services. Revoking photo permission does not automatically remove existing cleanup records.

Deletion and iCloud Photos

Swiping up or tapping Delete only marks an item. Deletion happens after batch review and system confirmation. Cancelling or a failed deletion keeps the pending list. The app does not download iCloud originals. If iCloud Photos is enabled, the system may sync deletions to other devices. Recovery availability and deadlines are shown in Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.

Sharing

Only when you initiate sharing does the app prepare local media and open the system share sheet. You choose the destination and sending action. Live Photos are shared as still images. Shared files may retain metadata, so check before sending. Temporary files for the operation are cleaned up when sharing finishes or is cancelled; force-quitting may leave files for the system to clean up. Receiving apps and services handle the content under their own policies.

Support communications

Contact 574700790@qq.com for support and privacy questions. The app's feedback button copies a contact address without sending mail. If an older version displays another address, use the current email published on this page. Information and contact details you voluntarily email are used to handle your request. Avoid unnecessary sensitive data. You may request access to or deletion of personal information in your support emails; necessary service, security, or legal records may need to be retained. Email providers process communications under their policies.

This website and policy updates

This site has no analytics, advertising tools, or photo uploads. Our hosting service, GitHub Pages, processes network request information needed to serve pages, such as IP addresses and browser request information. This is separate from the app's on-device photo processing. Changes to our practices will be reflected in this policy, the in-app policy, and our App Store disclosures, with an updated date.

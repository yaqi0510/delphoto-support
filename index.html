支持与反馈

一组一组，整理相册里的片刻。

片刻是一款适用于 iOS 18 及以上版本的 iPhone 相册整理工具。按图片、视频、分类或相册浏览本地内容，标记不需要的项目，再集中复核删除。

如何开始整理？

授权访问相册，在“图片”或“视频”页选择范围，点击“开始整理”。左右翻页，上滑标记删除；标记可以撤销。看完一组后复核清单，最后由系统确认删除。

为什么有些照片打不开？

片刻仅请求本地资源，不主动下载 iCloud 原片。只在云端保存、权限未覆盖或当前无法读取的项目可能被跳过。你可以先在系统照片 App 中下载需要的内容，再返回片刻。

误标记或删除后如何处理？

尚未确认删除时，可以撤销标记或在复核页保留内容。系统删除成功后，请查看照片 App 的“最近删除”；可恢复期限以系统显示为准。若开启 iCloud 照片，删除可能同步到其他设备。

如何修改相册权限？

在系统设置中找到片刻，调整照片访问权限；使用部分权限时，App 只能处理你选择的内容。片刻的图片和视频设置页也提供系统设置入口。

整理进度保存在哪里？

筛选、浏览进度、待删除标记及统计保存在本机。App 不设账号，也不向开发者上传图库或整理记录。更多信息请查看隐私政策。

Support

Review your photo library, one small batch at a time.

DelPhoto (片刻) is an iPhone photo and video organizer for iOS 18 or later. Browse local media, mark items, review the batch, and confirm deletion in the system dialog.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer, Yaqi Dai, at 574700790@qq.com. Include your app and iOS versions, steps to reproduce the issue, and any error message. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private media.

Local media and permissions

The app does not download iCloud originals. Cloud-only or inaccessible items may be skipped. Download an item in Apple Photos first if you want to review it locally. You can change photo access in iOS Settings at any time.

Deletion and recovery

Marking an item does not delete it. Review the batch and accept the system confirmation to delete. If iCloud Photos is enabled, the system may sync deletions to other devices. For recovery, check Recently Deleted in Apple Photos and the remaining time shown by the system.

Privacy

No account is required. Media and cleanup records are processed on your device. Read our privacy policy for details, including sharing and feedback.
